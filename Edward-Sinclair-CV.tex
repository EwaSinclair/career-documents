\section{KEY SKILLS \& COMPETENCIES}

\textbf{Problem Solving.}\enspace
I take a practical approach across hardware and software issues, working methodically from
root cause to solution. During my ME, a colleague was unable to activate the UAV chainsaw
through their app. On inspection, I identified that the app was not correctly transmitting
a WebSocket heartbeat, which the deadman switch required to enable the tool. Resolving this
fixed the issue and validated that my safety system was functioning as intended.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Adaptability \& Resilience.}\enspace
With a broad mechatronics background and strong emotional intelligence, I am confident in
shifting between hardware, software, support, and any other challenging tasks. I quickly
learned new tools and systems outside my work scope throughout my engineering honours
project, including telescope optics, TLE satellite tracking algorithms, and UI development,
all to support the project team and keep progress moving.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Communication.}\enspace
I communicate technical concepts clearly and adapt my delivery to the audience. At Basis,
when discrepancies emerged between coil behaviour on my test rig versus installed on PCBs,
I worked directly with the electrical team to diagnose the issue, walking them through my
test setup to identify the variable causing the discrepancy. I also presented my internship
project to the entire company using entertaining analogies for the non-technical audience.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Self-Management \& Motivation.}\enspace
I work well independently and manage my time effectively to meet goals. My self-motivation
is reflected in my 7.22/9.00 GPA and the positive feedback I have received from every
previous employer.
